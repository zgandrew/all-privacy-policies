Privacy Policy

App: BibleSchool for teacher
Developer: Bibleawr
Contact: info@bibleawr.com
Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what personal data the BibleSchool for teacher app ("App") processes, how it is used, and how it is stored. The App is intended exclusively for authorized teacher-missionaries of the Bibleawr organization.

1. Access to the App

The App is not public and does not have open registration. User accounts are created by an administrator of the Bibleawr organization and provided to teachers as ready-made credentials (login and password). Users do not enter or submit any personal data through the App during account creation.

2. Data the App Processes

Data Purpose Storage
Login credentials (login, password) Authentication in the App Password is not stored locally; JWT token is stored in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain)
FCM device token Sending push notifications On Bibleawr servers; deleted upon logout
Device information Trusted device management On Bibleawr servers

3. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Name or email via App forms (data is entered only by the administrator on the backend)
  • Geolocation
  • Contacts from the device address book
  • Photos or media files
  • Financial data or payment card details
  • Health data
  • Data about activity in other apps

4. How We Use Data

Collected data is used solely to operate the App: user authentication and sending service push notifications (e.g., about reviewed student lessons). We do not share data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Data Security

Data transmission between the App and the server is carried out via an encrypted HTTPS connection (TLS). The authorization JWT token is stored in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain) and is not accessible to other apps.

6. Push Notifications

The App uses Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) by Google to send push notifications. The FCM token is transmitted to the Bibleawr server and is used solely for delivering notifications from this App. Upon logout, the token is deleted from the server.

7. Third-Party Data (Students)

Through the App, a teacher can share links to lessons. By following such a link, an interested person (prospective student) may voluntarily submit their name and phone number on a separate web page outside of this App.

This data collection occurs outside the App, on the Bibleawr organization's website, and is governed by the privacy policy of that website. The App only displays already-collected data to the teacher for further communication with the student. The teacher is responsible for the appropriate use of this data in accordance with Bibleawr organization guidelines.

8. Cookies and Analytics

The App does not use cookies, trackers, or third-party analytics (Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, etc.).

9. Data Deletion

To delete your account and all associated data, please contact your organization administrator or email us at info@bibleawr.com. Data will be deleted within 30 days.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify users through the App or via the organization administrator. The date of the last update is indicated at the top of this document.
